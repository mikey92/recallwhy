# Examples (real runs, not edited)

Each request in `input/` was sent to `POST /run` on the API (`npm run api`) with live Zetaris, Meterless Nightshift and `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b`, on 25 Oct 2026, on the item master whose food lines are real products, in this order, starting from empty H-MEM and World Model state. `output/example_N.json` is the API's response, unchanged. `output/example_N/` holds the run's full `trace.jsonl`, its `report.html` / `report.md` (open the HTML in a browser), the briefs, the weekly `digest.md` and, for every recall with our stock, the action packet in `actions/<case>/` (pull list, supplier request, customer notice, `packet.json` with the SQL ids). The `trace` and `outDir` paths inside the JSON point at where the files were written in the working copy.

| Request | Response | What to look for in `trace.jsonl` (seq) |
|---|---|---|
| `example_1.json`: recalls since 2026-09-15, first 5 | 4 act-now, 1 needs-human | 3–18: the Cartographer reads Zetaris's catalog (`SHOW DATASOURCES`, `DESCRIBE DATASOURCE TABLE`, `SHOW LIGHTNING DATABASES`, …) and profiles the barcode columns. 27: its map — 6,810 of 14,244 barcoded ship notices join our item master as written, 11,809 without leading zeros. 46: the Scout's REST × JDBC join (live FDA feed × ERP UPCs). 392: the judge dismisses our real Taylor Farms "Salsa Fresca Medium 12 oz" while quoting "Tomato Salsa Fresca"; every product word of ours is in the quote, so the Auditor sends it to a person (`dismissal-same-line`). 427: the Auditor's barcode check misses rows, the Cartographer learns that SUP028 drops the UPC check digit, proves it on all 391 of SUP028's ship notices, writes the corrected map back to Zetaris as `reconciled.ship_notice_item_keys` and reads SUP028 back, 391 of 391; Zetaris's lineage (`INFER SQLTABLE SOURCES`, 423) and data-quality rule (`RUN DQ`, 425) confirm it: every row valid, 0 invalid. 449, 488, 538, 577: action packets (e.g. Taylor Fresh Foods' pico de gallo, H-1355-2026: pull 215 units in 4 stores). 581: the weekly digest through Markovian. |
| `example_2.json`: H-1362-2026 | 1 act-now | 81: SUP010 sends a 10-digit barcode core no rule in the code covers; the Cartographer shows Nemotron 3 Ultra sampled pairs and the model proposes a transform (ours `drop_first:1 → drop_last:1`). 92: SQL proves it on all 193 of SUP010's ship notices; it is written back to Zetaris, read back 193 of 193, and Zetaris's lineage (88) and data-quality rule (90) confirm it. 121: the action packet (424 units in 9 stores). |
| `example_3.json`: H-1099-2026 (Spring & Mulberry Lavender Rose chocolate) | 1 needs-human | 81, 95, 109: the judge dismisses our "Date-sweetened chocolate" while its own quote contains every product word of ours; the Auditor sends it back three times (`dismissal-names-our-item`) and 110 escalates the case to a person. Our real "Chocolate" (UPC 850055470019, printed in the notice) is in the same case, so it waits for the person too. |
| Human review, then `example_3.json` again → `example_3_after_human_decision.json` | 1 act-now | Between the runs, a reviewer recorded two decisions in H-MEM (`npm run decide`): NW-100165 *not this* ("our bar is the plain date-sweetened chocolate (its own UPC); the notice recalls the Lavender Rose flavour only") and NW-100125 *same* ("the notice prints our UPC 850055470019"). 57, 68: the Investigator follows both decisions without asking the model, and the brief says a reviewer decided. 104: the action packet. |
| `example_1.json` with `SAMPLE_MODE=true LLM_PROVIDER=none`, from empty state → `example_1_sample_mode.json` | 4 act-now, 1 needs-human | What a judge without Zetaris or Nightshift sees: the same agents and SQL on the DuckDB stand-in and no model calls. 395: the same Auditor → Cartographer correction (SUP028, 391 of 391). Action packets and the digest are written here too. |

The hosted NVIDIA endpoint answers about 4 calls a minute; the client paces `nvidia/*` calls and retries the rest (`llm.retry` events), and every call got through.
