RecallWhy

Five agents tell a retailer's product-safety manager which newly recalled products are on its shelves — which lots, which stores, how many customers to notify — across two live federal feeds, the ERP and the supplier portal, reached only through Zetaris. Every claim carries the query behind it.

One case
